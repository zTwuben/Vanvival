description=[h1]FAQ[/h1]
description=[h2]Q: Which vehicles can I start with?[/h2]
description=[i]A:[/i] Vanilla options and many supported mods.
description=[h2]Q: Are trailers supported?[/h2]
description=[i]A:[/i] Yes! There’s a [b]25% chance[/b] a trailer will spawn with your vehicle.
description=[h2]Q: Can I use this with other mods?[/h2]
description=[i]A:[/i] Presumably yes! I didn't overwrite any of the main functions of the game.
description=[h2]Q: What does the Nomad occupation do?[/h2]
description=[i]A:[/i] It gives you extra bonuses on some skills.
description=[h2]Q: Why am I getting a RVOwner trait detected but no RV mods enabled! Message[/h2]
description=[i]A:[/i] You've started with the RV Owner Trait, therefore you must enable at least one of the following mods:
description=- [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3488174689&searchtext=+%5BB42%2F1%5D+%2773+Winnebago+Chieftain][b][B42/1] '73 Winnebago Chieftain[/b][/url]
description=- [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2959472220][b]Filibuster Rhymes' Used Cars! - Animation Alpha[/b][/url]
description=- [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3217685049&searchtext=PZK+VLC+%28Vanilla+look-like+car+pack%29][b]PZK VLC (Vanilla look-like car pack)[/b][/url]
description=[h2]Q: Why am I getting a Trucker profession detected but Truck Mod not enabled! Message[/h2]
description=[i]A:[/i] You've started with the Trucker Occupation, therefore you must enable one of the following mods:
description=- [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3409472393&searchtext=W900+Semi-Truck+%5BB42%5D][b]W900 Semi-Truck [B42][/b][/url]
description=- [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3403314193&searchtext=Autotsar+Tuning+Atelier+-+Petyarbuilt+379+%5BB42%5D][b]Autotsar Tuning Atelier - Petyarbuilt 379 [B42][/b] - Only compatible with RV Interiors for now[/url]
description=[h2]Q: What does the Van Survivor trait do?[/h2]
description=[i]A:[/i] It gives you a preset loadout that spawns in your vehicle trunk instead of the randomly generated loot.
description=[h2]Q: Can you add these vehicles?[/h2]
description=[i]A:[/i] Since this mod builds on [b]RV Interiors by Mickey Knox[/b] and [b]RV Life by Bucho Jefe & thepelona[/b], I can only add vehicles that are compatible with those.
description=[h2]Q: Where can I report a bug?[/h2]
description=[i]A:[/i] There's should be a Bug Report discussion pinned, or feel free to post a comment, I tend to reply quickly.
description=[h2]Q: Is this mod fully developped?[/h2]
description=[i]A:[/i] Not yet, I intend to add more content to it. So be on the lookout for future updates.
